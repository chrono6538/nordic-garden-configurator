How Nordic compares — price & what's actually included

Every rival below links to its own product page so you can check the spec with your own eyes. The trap in this market is the headline number: many "comparable" prices are for uninsulated, single-glazed or self-build buildings, with insulation and fitting sold as extras.

Prices as advertised, incl. VAT · compiled 11 August 2026 · Nordic = Ultimate Package (building + roof upgrade + vinyl floor + concrete base, all fitted)

⚠️ Read the price with the spec — the common traps

  • Malvern — Studio PavilionHeadline price is an uninsulated glazed studio — insulation is a paid upgrade and the base is DIY.
  • Lillevilla — All modelsDouble-glazed but a bare self-build kit: insulation is £2,300–£3,509 extra and installation is £600–£3,000 extra.
  • Dunster House — TerminatorA single-glazed, uninsulated log cabin — supplied as a DIY kit.
  • Tiger Sheds — The BlakeNot insulated; double glazing is a paid upgrade. DIY / optional install.
  • BillyOh — WinchesterSingle-glazed, uninsulated cabin; double glazing +£383. Self-build.

What the badges mean: ✓ standard included in the quoted price · EXTRA / DIY / ✗ costs more or isn't offered. Nordic quotes are all-inclusive and fitted; that's why Nordic sits at the premium end on £/m² — you're comparing a finished, insulated, installed building against, in several cases, a flat-pack shell.
