What the deep dive shows

  • Spec them up and the gap closes. Add what NGB includes as standard and the genuinely-comparable rivals land right around NGB per m²; the ones that undercut do it by dropping the concrete base or the maintenance-free skin.
  • Cheap headlines aren't like-for-like. Malvern "from £4,490" is an uninsulated timber shell (own specced RRP £16.8k–£38.2k + a base it doesn't supply). Green Retreats' B-Range is "building+install only"; its own published options lift it to ~£15.2k–£28.3k.
  • Only NGB (uPVC), Booths (steel/uPVC) and Green Retreats X1 (metal) are genuinely maintenance-free. Cabin Master, eDEN, Crown and Green Retreats G/A are timber needing periodic treatment.
